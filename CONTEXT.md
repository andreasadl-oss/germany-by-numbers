# Germany by Numbers / Deutschland nach Zahlen

A public website of charts about Germany, built from official open data and free to reuse.

## Language

**Germany**:
The territory of unified Germany (since 3 October 1990). National series start in 1991 unless a source reconstructs today's borders for earlier years.
_Avoid_: Federal Republic (ambiguous before 1990), West Germany

**Land** (plural **Länder**):
One of the 16 federal states, identified by its NUTS 1 code (DE1–DEG).
_Avoid_: state, Bundesland in code, region

**Topic**:
A group of related indicators shown on one page (e.g. Energy & climate).
_Avoid_: category, section

**Indicator**:
One measured quantity with a source, unit and chart, e.g. "Electricity generation by source". The unit everything else is organised around.
_Avoid_: chart (a chart is how an indicator is drawn), dataset, metric

**Series**:
One line, bar or band within an indicator, e.g. "Wind" within electricity generation.
_Avoid_: variable, column

**Source**:
The organisation that publishes the data an indicator uses, credited on every chart.
_Avoid_: provider

**Refresh**:
An automated run that re-downloads every indicator from its source and republishes the site.
_Avoid_: update, sync
